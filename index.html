FAQ

Still Have Questions?

Answers to questions customers frequently ask

What's the difference between Takaful and regular Insurance?

Takaful is based on Islamic Shariah principles — the concept of mutual assistance (tabarru'). Contributions are pooled together to help participants facing hardship. Any fund surplus is returned to participants. PruBSN Takaful is fully approved by Bank Negara Malaysia.

What is the Lady Plan and who should take it?

The Lady Plan is a special takaful plan for women with additional benefits covering women's cancers (including breast, cervical, and ovarian cancer), pregnancy complications, and surgery related to the reproductive system. Suitable for all adult women.

When should I register Prebirth for my baby?

The Prebirth plan (Asas360) can be registered from week 13 of pregnancy up to week 36. This plan protects the baby against birth complications, congenital defects, premature birth, and neonatal ICU treatment.

How much is the monthly contribution for a medical card?

Contributions start from as low as RM80/month, depending on age, health status, and the level of protection chosen. Contact me for a free quote based on your profile!

Can I register if I have a pre-existing condition?

It depends on the type and severity of the condition. Some pre-existing conditions can still be covered with an exclusion or a contribution loading. Whatever your situation, don't wait — meet me for a free assessment!
